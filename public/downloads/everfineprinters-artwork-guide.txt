EverfinePrinters — Artwork Guidelines
======================================

READY TO PRINT?

General guidelines for preparing your artwork:

1. High-Resolution Artwork
   Submit files at 300 DPI or higher for crisp, sharp printing.

2. CMYK Color Mode
   Use CMYK where appropriate — RGB files may print with color shifts.

3. Bleed
   Include bleed where required so edge-to-edge designs print cleanly.

4. Safe Margins
   Keep important text and logos within the safe margin area.

5. Proper Fonts
   Embed or outline fonts to avoid substitution issues.

6. Print-Ready PDF
   Export a print-ready PDF where appropriate for the most reliable results.

For product-specific specifications (bleed size, trim size, color profiles),
please contact EverfinePrinters directly or request a quote through the website.
